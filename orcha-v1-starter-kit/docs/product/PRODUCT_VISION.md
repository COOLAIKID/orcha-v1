# Orcha Product Vision

Orcha turns an outcome into a persistent AI company. The consumer does not manage agents; they own a Company with an objective, a team, a workspace, a timeline, experiments, assets, and measurable progress.

The underlying system remains powerful and developer-oriented. It is surfaced as **Orcha Studio**, where advanced users can inspect agents, prompts, tools, model routing, workflows, files, permissions, logs, and VM state.

## Product pillars

1. **Outcome before automation.** Work is always connected to a company objective and milestone.
2. **Persistent company.** The company keeps memory, artifacts, tasks, decisions, and runtime state.
3. **Visible momentum.** Live HQ turns real events into understandable activity and progress.
4. **Measured evolution.** Variants compete on explicit metrics; winners are promoted, losers are retired, and every change is reversible.
5. **Autonomy with boundaries.** The company acts without needless prompts, but capability grants, budgets, file tiers, and consequential-action gates remain enforceable.

## Consumer promise

“Tell Orcha what you want accomplished. It will make a plan, build the team, work continuously, show you what happened, and improve the approach when the evidence says it should.”

## Target first user

A technically curious founder, operator, or maker who wants a small software product built and operated without learning multi-agent infrastructure.
